## What it's for
Insights lists findings Trenova computes from your own records when service, cash, cost or compliance moves in the wrong direction, such as on-time performance slipping, money earned but not yet collected, or cost the operation is losing quietly. Each card shows its urgency, its area, the figures that triggered it, and a recommendation. The figures always come from your data; where an explanation was written by AI, the card says so. Operations, billing and management staff use it to spot problems and jump to the records behind them.

## Tasks

### Review open findings
Keywords: what's wrong, problems, anomalies, alerts
1. Open [Insights](/insights). **Open** findings show by default.
2. Narrow the list with the **Area** chips (**Service**, **Cash**, **Cost**, **Compliance**) and the **Urgency** chips (Critical, Warning, Info).
3. Select a finding's headline to open its details: **What was measured**, the **Explanation**, **Since last time** (whether it is getting worse, improving or holding steady), and **Why you are seeing this**.

### Go to the records behind a finding
Keywords: drill down, which loads, which invoices
1. Open [Insights](/insights) and select the finding's headline.
2. Under **The records behind this**, select a link to open the matching records on their own page.

### Dismiss a finding
Keywords: ignore insight, hide finding, not relevant
1. Open [Insights](/insights).
2. Select **Dismiss** on the card, or open the finding, optionally say why under **Not worth acting on?**, and select **Dismiss for 30 days**.

### Bring back a dismissed finding
Keywords: undo dismiss, restore insight
1. Open [Insights](/insights) and select the **Dismissed** status chip.
2. Select **Restore** on the card, or open it and select **Restore this finding**.

## Notes
Needs read access to insights.

A dismissed finding comes back if its condition is still there after 30 days. **Closed** holds findings a later refresh no longer makes. A card marked **Numbers may be out of date** has not been refreshed recently.
