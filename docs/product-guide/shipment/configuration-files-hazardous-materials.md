## What it's for
The hazardous materials list holds the hazmat records your organization ships: name, DOT hazard class, UN number, packing group, proper shipping name, emergency contact and compliance flags such as placards and marine pollutants. A commodity can be linked to one of these records. Safety, compliance and operations staff maintain it.

## Tasks

### Add a hazardous material from the DOT reference
Keywords: new hazmat, UN number lookup, 49 CFR 172.101
1. Open [Hazardous materials](/shipment-management/configuration-files/hazardous-materials).
2. Select **New hazardous material**.
3. Type at least two characters of the material name in **Name** and pick a match from the list. The match fills **Name**, **Proper shipping name**, **Description**, **UN number**, **Class** and, where the reference has them, **Subsidiary hazard class**, **Special provisions** and **ERG guide number**.
4. Check the filled values and complete the rest: **Status**, **Packing group**, **Emergency contact**, **Emergency contact phone**, **Handling instructions** and the compliance switches (**Placard required**, **Marine pollutant**, **Inhalation hazard**, **Reportable quantity**).
5. Select **Save**.

### Edit a hazardous material
Keywords: update hazmat, change hazard class
1. Open [Hazardous materials](/shipment-management/configuration-files/hazardous-materials).
2. Select the row, or right-click it and choose **Edit**.
3. Change the fields and select **Save**. The **Code** is generated by the system and cannot be edited.

### Activate or deactivate several hazardous materials
Keywords: bulk status, inactivate hazmat
1. Open [Hazardous materials](/shipment-management/configuration-files/hazardous-materials).
2. Tick the rows to change.
3. Select **Update status** in the bar that appears and choose **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Notes
**Name**, **Status**, **Description** and **Class** are required. When **Reportable quantity** is on, **Reportable quantity threshold** is required too.

Adding needs create permission for hazardous materials; editing needs update permission.
