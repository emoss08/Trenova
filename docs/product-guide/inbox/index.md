## What it's for
The inbox shows mail that arrived on your organization's monitored addresses and what the desk (the AI assistant) made of each message: what kind of mail it is (tender, rate confirmation, proof of delivery, invoice, status request, detention dispute or other), which shipment or customer it is about, how sure it was, and a suggested next step. Customer service, dispatch and billing staff use it to work through the mail the desk could not settle on its own.

It reads like a mail client: folders on the left (**Waiting on you**, **Handled**, **Ignored**, **Everything**, then **By kind** and **Mailboxes**), the message list in the middle, and the open message on the right with its attachments, **What the desk made of it**, and **How it got here**.

## Tasks

### Work through mail waiting on you
Keywords: triage email, review inbound messages, clear inbox
1. Open [Inbox](/inbox) and select **Waiting on you**.
2. Select a message, or press j to open the first one and keep pressing j to go down the list (k goes back).
3. Read **What the desk made of it** and follow the **Suggested next** step if it fits, such as attaching a document to the shipment it names or opening that shipment.
4. Optionally note what you did in the box at the bottom, then select **Mark handled** (e) or **Ignore** (#). The next waiting message opens.

### Link a message to the right shipment or customer
Keywords: wrong shipment, match email, the desk could not tell, attach email to load
1. Open the message in [Inbox](/inbox).
2. Select **Link by hand** (or press l).
3. Choose the **Shipment** or **Customer**, fill in **Why this is the right record**, and select **Link**.

### Ask the desk to handle a message
Keywords: draft reply, build load from email, answer status request
1. Open the message in [Inbox](/inbox).
2. Select **Ask the desk** (or press a), or the suggested **Ask the desk to answer** / **Ask the desk to build it** button. A desk conversation opens about that message, where you can review what it drafts.

### Find a message
Keywords: search email, find by sender, find by subject
1. Open [Inbox](/inbox) and pick a folder, a kind under **By kind**, or a mailbox under **Mailboxes**.
2. Type in **Search sender or subject** (or press / to jump there). Search looks at the sender's name and address and the subject.

## Notes
Needs read access to inbound messages. Messages the desk could not make sense of are held back and also land in **Waiting on you**.

Monitored addresses are set up under [Inbound mailboxes](/admin/inbound-mailboxes); people who can manage them see a **Manage** link beside **Mailboxes**.

The folder, search and open message are kept in the page address, so a link to a message opens exactly that message.
