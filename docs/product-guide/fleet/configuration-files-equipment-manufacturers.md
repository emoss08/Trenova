## What it's for
Equipment manufacturers is the list of makers your tractors and trailers can be recorded
against, such as Freightliner or Great Dane. Fleet administrators maintain it; every tractor and
trailer must name one in its **Equip. manufacturer** field.

## Tasks

### Add an equipment manufacturer
Keywords: new manufacturer, add make, add OEM
1. Open [Equipment manufacturers](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-manufacturers).
2. Select **New equipment manufacturer**.
3. Fill in **Status** and **Name**, and optionally a **Description**.
4. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** or **Save & add another** from the save button's menu.

### Edit an equipment manufacturer
1. Open [Equipment manufacturers](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-manufacturers).
2. Find the manufacturer with the search box or **Filter**, then select its row.
3. Change the fields and select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Activate or deactivate manufacturers
1. Open [Equipment manufacturers](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-manufacturers).
2. Tick the rows you want to change.
3. Choose **Update status** in the bar at the bottom of the table and pick the status.

## Notes
- Viewing the page needs read access to equipment manufacturers; adding needs create access and
  editing or changing status needs update access.
