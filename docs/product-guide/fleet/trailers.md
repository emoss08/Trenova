## What it's for
Trailers lists every trailer in your fleet with its code, status, equipment type, manufacturer,
fleet code and last known location. Fleet and dispatch staff use it to add trailers, keep their
registration and inspection details current, change their status, and record where a trailer
actually is.

## Tasks

### Add a trailer
Keywords: new trailer, create trailer
1. Open [Trailers](/equipment/trailers).
2. Select **New trailer**.
3. Fill in **Status**, **Code**, **Equipment type**, **Equip. manufacturer**, **Model** and
   **Make**. These are required.
4. Optionally add **Year**, **Max load weight**, **Fleet code** and the
   **Registration information** (**VIN**, **Registration number**, **Samsara asset ID**,
   **Registration state**, **Registration expiry**, **License plate number**,
   **Last inspection date**).
5. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** or **Save & add another** from the save button's menu.

### Edit a trailer
Keywords: update trailer, change trailer details
1. Open [Trailers](/equipment/trailers).
2. Find the trailer with the search box or **Filter**, then select its row.
3. Change the fields on the **Details** tab, or use the **Documents** tab to view and **Upload**
   files for the trailer.
4. Select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Record where a trailer is
Keywords: locate trailer, move trailer, reposition, trailer location, drop yard
1. Open [Trailers](/equipment/trailers).
2. Right-click the trailer's row and choose **Locate trailer**.
3. Pick the **New location**, then select **Locate trailer**.

### Change a trailer's status
Keywords: out of service, at maintenance, mark sold, available
1. Open [Trailers](/equipment/trailers).
2. Select the status badge in the trailer's **Status** column and pick the new status.
3. To change several at once, tick the rows, then choose **Update status** in the bar at the
   bottom of the table and pick the status.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to trailers and the asset operations feature. Adding a trailer needs
  create access; editing, locating and status changes need update access.
- Locating a trailer creates and completes an empty reposition move to the new location
  automatically, so the trailer's **Last known location** updates.
