## What it's for
Equipment types are the categories your tractors, trailers and containers are sorted into, such
as a 53-foot dry van or a reefer. Each type has a code, a class (Tractor, Trailer, Container or
Other), an optional colour and an interior length. Fleet administrators maintain the list; every
tractor and trailer must point at one.

## Tasks

### Add an equipment type
Keywords: new equipment type, create trailer type
1. Open [Equipment types](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-types).
2. Select **New equipment type**.
3. Fill in **Status**, **Code** and **Class**. These are required.
4. Optionally add a **Description**, a **Color** and the **Interior Length (ft)**.
5. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** or **Save & add another** from the save button's menu.

### Edit an equipment type
1. Open [Equipment types](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-types).
2. Find the type with the search box or **Filter**, then select its row.
3. Change the fields and select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Activate or deactivate equipment types
Keywords: retire equipment type, inactive
1. Open [Equipment types](/equipment/configuration-files/equipment-types).
2. Tick the rows you want to change.
3. Choose **Update status** in the bar at the bottom of the table and pick the status.

## Notes
- Viewing the page needs read access to equipment types; adding needs create access and editing
  or changing status needs update access.
