## What it's for
The welcome screen is the first thing a new Trenova Cloud organization sees after its owner
verifies their email. **Nova**, a scripted setup guide, asks for the company profile, how the
company moves freight and whether to load sample data, one question at a time, then creates the
workspace and opens the app. Nova is not AI; every line is fixed text. Until setup is finished,
every page sends you back here. It never appears on a self-hosted installation.

## Tasks

### Finish setting up a new organization
Keywords: complete onboarding, company profile, first time setup, Nova
1. Confirm the **Company name** (it is filled in from signup) and press Enter.
2. Pick the timezone dispatch, appointments and reports should use. Your browser's zone is listed
   first and already selected; press Enter to keep it, or press its number. Choose
   **Other timezone…** for a zone outside the US.
3. Enter the **Street address**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP** of your headquarters and select
   **Continue**.
4. Add your **SCAC code** and **USDOT number** if you have them, or select **Skip for now**.
5. Choose how the company moves freight: **Asset carrier**, **Freight brokerage** or **Both**.
6. Choose **Load sample data** to start with a few customers, locations, equipment and shipments,
   or **Start empty**.
7. Check the summary and select **Looks good, finish setup** (or press Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter on a
   Mac). Nova narrates the setup while the workspace is created; then select **Open Trenova**.

### Change an answer before finishing
Keywords: go back, edit setup, fix company name, typo
1. Select **Edit** beside one of your answers, or select a line in the summary. That question
   opens again with your answer filled in.
2. Change it and answer again. You return straight to where you were.
3. To walk back one question at a time, press Esc or select **Back** in the top-left corner.

### If setup fails
Keywords: setup error, onboarding failed, try again
1. If the server refuses an answer, Nova goes back to that question and shows why. Fix it and
   continue to the summary again.
2. If something else went wrong, select **Try again**. Nothing you entered is lost.

## Notes
Everything set here can be changed later in
[Organization settings](/admin/organization-settings). Sample records count toward the free demo's
limits like any other record and can be deleted at any time; see [Plan & usage](/admin/plan-usage).
