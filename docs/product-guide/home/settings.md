## What it's for
Settings is where you change things about your own account: your profile and availability, how Trenova looks and keeps time for you, what notifications reach you, and your password, second factor and signed-in sessions. It is yours alone; what applies to the whole organization is in the organization's own settings.

The page is one long scroll in four sections, **Profile**, **Preferences**, **Notifications** and **Security**, listed down the left beside the module rail; select one to jump to it, or search for a setting in the box above them. Each change saves as you make it, and **Undo** in the confirmation puts it back. Open Settings from your name at the bottom of the rail, from the command palette, or with Ctrl+Shift+S (⌘⇧S on a Mac) from any page.

## Tasks

### Let your team know you are away
Keywords: out of office, vacation, focus mode, do not disturb, availability, hand off
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Profile**.
2. Under **Availability**, choose **Focus** to hold everything but urgent notifications and direct @mentions for a while, or **Away**.
3. For **Away**, set **Back on**, write an **Away note** if you like, and pick who to **Hand off to**, or **No one, hold them for me**.
4. Choose **Available** when you are back.

### Change your theme, language or time zone
Keywords: dark mode, light mode, 24-hour clock, timezone, language, reduce motion, vim keys
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Preferences**.
2. Change **Theme**, **Language**, **Time zone** or **Time format**. **Reduce motion** cuts slides and other movement. **Vim keys in the formula editor** gives the formula editor Vim's modes and AstroNvim's keys.

### Hide the assistant
Keywords: turn off the assistant, hide Nova, remove the AI button, disable AI chat, Ctrl+J
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Preferences**.
2. Turn off **Show the assistant**. Its button, the panel and the Ctrl+J shortcut leave every page, on every device you sign in from, until you turn it back on. The Desk and asking about a record from the command palette still work.

### Choose which notifications reach you
Keywords: email notifications, desktop notifications, digest, quiet hours, mute, pause notifications
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Notifications**.
2. Under **Delivery**, choose what reaches you on the **Desktop** and by **Email**.
3. Turn on **Quiet hours** to hold notifications at set times; **Let urgent ones through** keeps urgent ones coming.
4. Use **Pause notifications** to stop desktop notifications and emails for a while.
5. Under **What you hear about**, turn off anything you don't need. Those marked **Required** are set by your organization.

### Change your password
Keywords: reset password, new password, update password
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Security**.
2. Select **Change password**, enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
3. Select **Update password**.

### Turn on two-factor authentication
Keywords: 2FA, MFA, authenticator app, recovery codes
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Security**.
2. Beside **Two-factor authentication**, select **Set up** and follow the steps with your authenticator app.

### Sign out a device you no longer use
Keywords: sessions, signed-in devices, lost laptop, sign out everywhere
1. Open [Settings](/settings) and go to **Security**.
2. Under **Where you're signed in**, select **Sign out** beside the session, or **Sign out everywhere else** to end every session but this one.

## Notes
The link at the foot of a notification email opens the **Notifications** section. Your display name and email address are managed by your organization; ask an admin to change them.
