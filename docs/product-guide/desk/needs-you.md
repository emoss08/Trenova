## What it's for
Needs you is everything waiting on people across the operation, in one list with one row per record: a shipment's late warning from the Watchtower, the customer's email about it in Triage and the plan an agent drafted to recover it in Decisions are one row. Rows are grouped by how soon their deadline is (**Act now**, **Today**, **Coming up** and the rest), and the line under each title names the record and the queues that have something about it. Open it from **Needs you** in the [Desk](/desk) rail, or press G then N.

**Watchtower**, **Triage** and **Decisions** are lenses on the list, not separate places: pick one to see only the rows with something in that queue. Each row still shows every signal about its record, and each lens's own page stays a step away for the deep work. You only see what the Watchtower would show you: items whose records you may not open are left out, and a record you may not read is counted, never named.

## Tasks

### Work what needs you
Keywords: needs you, what needs me, everything waiting, one list, lenses, watchtower lens, triage lens, decisions lens
1. Open [Needs you](/desk/needs) from the rail, or press G then N.
2. Each row is one record and everything waiting about it, grouped by how soon its deadline is: **Act now**, **Today**, **Coming up** and the rest. The line under the title names the record and the queues that have something about it.
3. Pick **Everything**, **Watchtower**, **Triage** or **Decisions** above the list (or press 1 to 4) to see only rows with something in that queue. Each row still shows all its signals.
4. Select a row. The top of the page names the record, offers **Open the case** and **Open the record**, and lists every signal about it; select a signal to read it in full.
5. Act on the signal in front of you with the bar at the bottom, or the same keys as the Watchtower: J and K move, E marks it done, S snoozes it until tomorrow morning, and H hands it to an agent. A decision opens on [Decisions](/desk/decisions) with **Review**, where its dry run is read; a message opens in Triage.

### Open the record behind a row
Keywords: open case, open record, record page
1. Select the row in [Needs you](/desk/needs).
2. Select **Open the case** to see the record's card beside everything open about it, or **Open the record** to open its own page.
