## What it's for
Dash control decides what drivers can see and do in the Dash driver portal. The page is one
settings form in five cards: **Load workflow** (accepting loads, arrive and depart, paperwork and
messaging), **Pay visibility** (per-load pay before settlement), **Expenses & disputes**, **Profile
self-Service** (what drivers can update on their own record) and **Reminders & alerts** (credential
reminders and detention alerts).

Operations, safety and payroll managers use it. Changes reach drivers as soon as they are saved.

## Tasks

### Choose what drivers can do on their loads
Keywords: accept loads, decline loads, forced dispatch, POD upload, arrive depart
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. In **Load workflow**, turn on **Load acceptance** so drivers accept or decline new assignments,
   and **Allow declines** if they may decline with a reason.
3. Turn **Self-Service arrive / depart**, **POD / BOL upload** and **Load messaging** on or off.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Show or hide pay before settlement
Keywords: load pay, pay estimate, driver pay visibility
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. In **Pay visibility**, turn on **Per-load pay** to show what each load pays, and **Pay
   estimates** to show an estimated payout on active loads.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Let drivers submit expenses and disputes
Keywords: reimbursement, lumper, receipts, settlement dispute
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. In **Expenses & disputes**, turn on **Expense submission** and, if receipts are needed, **Require
   receipts**.
3. Turn on **Settlement disputes** to let drivers flag a statement or line for review.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Control driver profile self-service
Keywords: update phone, address change, upload CDL, time off request
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. In **Profile self-Service**, turn on **Qualification document upload**, **Contact info edits**
   and **Time-off requests** as needed.
3. Turn on **Approve contact edits** to hold contact changes for office approval.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Set up credential reminders and detention alerts
Keywords: license expiry reminder, medical card reminder, dwell alert
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. In **Reminders & alerts**, turn on **Credential expiry reminders** and choose **How drivers are
   told**: one notice per obligation, a daily round-up or a weekly round-up. For the weekly
   round-up, pick the **Weekly round-up day**.
3. Turn on **Detention alerts** and enter the **Detention Threshold (minutes)**.
4. Select **Save changes**.

## Notes
The page is only available to organizations with asset operations turned on. Opening it needs read
access to Dash control; saving needs update access. Settlement statements are always visible to
drivers, and compliance dates such as CDL and medical expiry are always set by the office. **Allow
declines** needs **Load acceptance**, **Pay estimates** needs **Per-load pay**, and **Require
receipts** needs **Expense submission**.
