## What it's for
Dash control decides what drivers can see and do in the Dash driver portal. It is one settings
form, saved once, in tabs: **Loads** (accepting assignments, arrivals and departures, paperwork,
messages and detention alerts), **Pay & expenses** (pay before settlement, expense claims and
settlement disputes; only on a plan with driver settlements), **Profile & time off** (what drivers
update on their own record) and **Reminders** (due-date reminders for credentials and training;
only on a plan with compliance or training).

Operations, safety and payroll managers use it. Changes reach drivers as soon as they are saved.
When a save is refused, the tab holding the setting to fix opens and is marked.

## Tasks

### Choose what drivers can do on their loads
Keywords: accept loads, decline loads, forced dispatch, POD upload, BOL upload, arrive depart
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Loads**, under **Assignments**, turn on **Accept assignments** so drivers confirm each new
   load. **Allow declines** appears beneath it; turn it on if drivers may turn a load down with a
   reason.
3. Under **On the road**, turn **Arrivals and departures**, **Load paperwork** and **Load messages**
   on or off.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Alert dispatch to detention
Keywords: dwell alert, detention threshold, free time
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Loads**, under **Detention**, turn on **Detention alerts** and enter the **Threshold** in
   minutes, from 15 minutes to a day. Two hours is the usual free time.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Show or hide pay before settlement
Keywords: load pay, pay estimate, driver pay visibility
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Pay & expenses**, turn on **Pay per load** to show what each load pays. **Pay estimates**
   appears beneath it; turn it on to show an estimate on active loads.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Let drivers claim expenses and dispute settlements
Keywords: reimbursement, lumper, receipts, settlement dispute
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Pay & expenses**, turn on **Expense claims** and, if receipts are needed, **Require a
   receipt** beneath it.
3. Turn on **Settlement disputes** to let drivers question a statement or one of its lines.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Control driver profile self-service
Keywords: update phone, address change, upload CDL, time off request
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Profile & time off**, turn on **Qualification documents** and **Contact details** as
   needed. **Approve changes** appears under **Contact details**; turn it on to hold contact
   changes for office approval.
3. Turn on **Time-off requests** to let drivers ask for time off from Dash.
4. Select **Save changes**.

### Set up due-date reminders
Keywords: license expiry reminder, medical card reminder, training reminder, round-up, digest
1. Open [Dash control](/admin/dash-control).
2. On **Reminders**, turn on **Remind drivers** and choose **How often**: one notice per item, a
   daily round-up or a weekly round-up. For the weekly round-up, pick the **Round-up day**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

## Notes
The page is only available to organizations with asset operations turned on. Opening it needs read
access to Dash control; saving needs update access. Settlement statements are always visible to
drivers, and license and medical dates are always set by the office. A setting that only applies
under another is hidden while that one is off. **Allow declines**, **Pay estimates** and **Require a
receipt** are saved off then, and turning **Remind drivers** off returns **How often** to one notice
per item.
