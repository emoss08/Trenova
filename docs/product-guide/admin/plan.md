## What it's for
On a Managed Trenova instance, Trenova sells a base plan and add-ons such as Dispatch
Intelligence or Trenova AI. This page shows an administrator what the plan includes, each
add-on on the plan and what it adds, and each add-on that is not on it and what it would add.
Nothing on this page changes the plan: add or drop an add-on from your Trenova account.

**Recent changes** lists when an add-on joined or left the plan. When an add-on leaves, its pages
and actions are hidden and its background work stops, but everything you entered with it is kept
and comes back if you add it again. Administrators are also notified each time an add-on joins or
leaves.

## Tasks

### See what your plan includes
Keywords: subscription, which features do we have, what are we paying for
1. Open [Your plan](/admin/plan).
2. Read **Included in your plan** and **Add-ons on your plan**.

### See what an add-on would add
Keywords: upgrade, buy an add-on, more features
1. Open [Your plan](/admin/plan).
2. Read **Add-ons you can add**. Add one from your Trenova account.

### See when an add-on left the plan
Keywords: dropped add-on, feature disappeared, missing page
1. Open [Your plan](/admin/plan).
2. Read **Recent changes**.

## Notes
Needs permission to update the organization. The page exists only on a Managed Trenova instance;
a self-hosted install has every feature.
