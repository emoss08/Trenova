## What it's for
The OSHA 300 log lists the recordable work-related injuries and illnesses for a year (Form 300)
and the year's 300A summary. Figures at the top give **Recordable cases**, the **Incident rate**,
the **DART rate** and **Days lost**. The case table shows each case's **Case** number,
**Employee**, **Date**, **Where it happened**, **What happened**, log **Column**, days
**Away** and **Restricted**, **Type** and **Status**. Privacy cases have the name withheld from
the posted log.

The totals are counted from the log every time it is read, so correcting a case later never
leaves an out-of-date summary. Safety and HR staff use the page to keep the log and to prepare,
certify and post the 300A.

## Tasks

### Record a new injury or illness
Keywords: add OSHA case, report injury, workplace accident
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers), select the worker and go to the **Safety** tab.
2. Under **Injuries & illnesses**, select **Record a case**.
3. Fill in the dates, **What happened**, **Where**, **Body part**, the **Injury or illness** type,
   **Treatment given**, days away or restricted, and the **OSHA classification**, then save. A
   recordable case appears on this log for the year it happened.

### Find and review a case
Keywords: look up injury case, case details
1. Open [OSHA 300 log](/hr/osha-log) and pick the year.
2. Filter the cases or search by name, injury or place.
3. Open the row's actions menu and choose **Open case** to see the full case, including any
   workers' compensation claim, or **Open worker** to go to the worker's safety tab.

### Correct or delete a case
Keywords: edit OSHA case, fix injury record, remove case
1. Open [OSHA 300 log](/hr/osha-log) and find the case.
2. From the row's actions menu choose **Edit case**, change the details and save.
3. Or choose **Delete case** and confirm with **Delete case**. The case number is not reused.

### Prepare and certify the 300A summary
Keywords: OSHA 300A, annual summary, certify summary, post summary
1. Open [OSHA 300 log](/hr/osha-log) and pick the year.
2. On the summary card, select **Start the summary** (or **Edit figures**) and fill in
   **Annual average employees**, **Total hours worked**, **NAICS code**, the
   **Certifying executive** with their **Title** and **Phone**, and, once filed,
   **Electronically submitted** and the **Submission reference**. Select **Save**.
3. Select **Certify**. Post the summary where employees can read it, from February 1 to April 30.
4. To correct a certified summary, select **Reopen**.

## Notes
- The page appears only for organizations that run their own assets (asset operations) and needs
  read access to worker injuries. Editing cases and the summary figures needs update access,
  deleting needs delete access, and certifying or reopening needs manage access.
- **Certify** stays unavailable while something the summary needs is missing; the card says what.
- The incident and DART rates need the hours worked from the 300A figures.
