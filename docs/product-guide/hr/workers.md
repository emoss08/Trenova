## What it's for
Workers is the roster of every driver and employee, with their status, tenure, worker type,
driver type, fleet code and at-a-glance compliance, training, safety and drug and alcohol
standing. HR, safety and operations staff use it to add people, keep their records current and
manage paid time off. The **Paid time off** tab lists every time-off request so managers can
approve, reject or cancel them, above a chart of approved time off and a list of requested PTO.

Selecting a worker opens their record on the **Overview** tab. The other tabs hold the rest of
their file: **General information**, **Employment information** (with its **History** of
employment events), **Compliance status**, **Credentials**, **Checklist**, **Training**,
**Safety**, **Testing** (drug and alcohol tests and Clearinghouse queries), **DQ file**,
**Reviews**, **HOS**, **Pay**, **Time off**, **Schedule**, **Leave**, **Documents** and
**Portal** (their Dash sign-in).

## Tasks

### Add a worker
Keywords: new driver, hire, create employee, onboard driver
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers).
2. Select **New worker**.
3. On **General information**, fill in **Status**, **Worker type**, **First name**,
   **Last name**, **Driver type**, the address and **Phone number**.
4. On **Employment information**, enter the **Date of birth**, **Hire date**,
   **License number** and **License expiry**, plus any CDL and medical details.
5. On **Compliance status**, set the compliance and availability fields if you know them.
6. Select **Create worker**.

### Update a worker's record
Keywords: edit driver, change driver type, deactivate worker, terminate
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers).
2. Find the worker with the search box or **Filter**, then select their row.
3. Go to **General information**, **Employment information** or **Compliance status** and
   change the fields.
4. Select **Save** or **Save & close**.
5. To change several workers at once, tick their rows and use **Update status**,
   **Update type** or **Update driver type** in the bar at the bottom of the table.

### Add or renew a credential
Keywords: CDL, medical card, TWIC, hazmat endorsement, licence, certificate
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers) and select the worker's row.
2. Go to the **Credentials** tab.
3. Select **Add credential**, or open the actions menu on an existing credential and choose
   its renew, edit, verify or archive option.
4. Pick the **Credential type**, fill in **Number**, **Issuing authority**, **Issued** and
   **Expires**, and attach a scan of the document.
5. Select **Add credential** (or **Renew credential** when renewing).

### Assign training
Keywords: training course, required training, refresher, orientation
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers).
2. Tick the workers who need the training and choose **Assign training** in the bar at the
   bottom of the table.
3. Pick the **Courses**, optionally a **Due date** and **Notes**, then confirm.
4. For one worker, open their row, go to **Training**, and use **Assign course** or
   **Assign required**. Use **Record result** or **Waive** on a course to close it out.

### Request time off for a worker
Keywords: PTO, vacation, day off, leave request, book time off
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers) and select the **Paid time off** tab.
2. Select the New worker PTO button above the table. You can also open the worker's row, go to **Time off** and
   select **Request PTO**.
3. Pick the **Worker** and **Type**, set the **First day** and **Last day**, and add a
   **Reason**.
4. Select **Request PTO**. The request waits for approval.

### Approve or reject time off
Keywords: PTO approval, deny vacation, cancel time off
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers) and select the **Paid time off** tab.
2. Tick the requests, then choose **Approve**, **Reject** or **Cancel** in the bar at the bottom
   of the table. You can also right-click a single request.
3. Confirm the action, adding a reason where asked.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to workers and the asset operations feature. Adding a worker needs
  create access to workers.
- Each tab has its own permissions: verifying and archiving credentials, assigning training,
  recording safety events, and approving, rejecting or cancelling time off are only offered to
  people allowed to do them.
- The **HOS** tab and hours-of-service data come from Samsara; if it is not connected the tab
  says **Samsara telematics is not connected**.
- On the **Safety** tab, **Record event** logs an accident, citation or inspection,
  **Issue action** adds a step on the **Discipline ladder**, and **Add recognition** records
  kudos that can be shared to the driver's Dash.
