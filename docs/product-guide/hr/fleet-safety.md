## What it's for
Fleet safety reads the fleet the way a safety director does. The **Fleet** tab shows **Drivers**
by rating, **Average score**, **Events in window** and **Out of service** orders, then the seven
**CSA BASICs** with their weighted scores, **Events by month** with the trend, a breakdown
**By terminal**, and two driver lists: **Needs attention** (most active points first) and
**Best records**. The **My DOT** tab shows your own carrier's DOT profile as pulled from a connected
carrier intelligence provider.

Safety managers use the page to see where risk sits and which terminals and drivers to work with.
The figures are built from the safety events recorded on each worker.

## Tasks

### Review fleet safety
Keywords: CSA BASIC scores, safety trend, at-risk drivers
1. Open [Fleet safety](/hr/fleet-safety) on the **Fleet** tab.
2. Choose the counting window: **3 months**, **6 months**, **12 months** or **24 months**.
3. Read the **CSA BASICs** card (select **How the score is made** for the method) and the
   **Events by month** trend.

### Narrow to one terminal
Keywords: terminal safety, fleet code safety
1. Open [Fleet safety](/hr/fleet-safety).
2. Select a terminal under **By terminal**. The whole page narrows to it.
3. Select the terminal chip beside the window selector to go back to every terminal.

### Look at a driver's safety record
Keywords: driver points, driver safety events
1. Open [Fleet safety](/hr/fleet-safety).
2. Select a driver under **Needs attention** or **Best records**. Their worker record opens on the
   safety tab.

### Check or refresh your DOT profile
Keywords: FMCSA snapshot, carrier profile, my DOT number
1. Open [Fleet safety](/hr/fleet-safety) and select the **My DOT** tab.
2. Select **Refresh** to pull a new copy, or **Pull profile** if it has never been pulled.
3. If the tab says **Your organization has no DOT number**, select **Open organization settings**
   and add it. If **No carrier intelligence provider is connected**, select
   **Open integrations** to connect one.

## Notes
- The page appears only for organizations that run their own assets (asset operations). The
  **Fleet** tab needs read access to worker safety events; the **My DOT** tab needs read access to
  carrier intelligence, and refreshing needs update access.
- Points roll off two years after the event.
