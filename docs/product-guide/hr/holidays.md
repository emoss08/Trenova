## What it's for
The holiday calendar shows the year month by month with your company holidays and blackout dates
marked, and lists every date below the calendar. HR administrators keep it up to date. Holidays
are not charged against a time-off request under a PTO policy that skips weekends; blackout dates
cannot be requested off at all.

## Tasks

### Add a holiday or blackout date
Keywords: new holiday, block out dates, freeze time off, peak season blackout
1. Open [Holiday calendar](/hr/holidays).
2. Select **Add date**, or select an empty day on the calendar to start with that date filled in.
3. Fill in **Name** and **Date**, and choose the **Kind**: a holiday, or a blackout that no one
   can request off.
4. Turn on **Repeats every year** for dates that recur annually, and optionally add a **Note**.
5. Select **Add date**.

### Change or remove a date
Keywords: edit holiday, delete holiday, remove blackout
1. Open [Holiday calendar](/hr/holidays).
2. Select the marked day on the calendar, or the edit button beside the date in the list below.
3. Change the fields and select **Save changes**.
4. To delete it, select the remove button beside the date in the list, then **Remove** to
   confirm, or **Keep** to leave it.

### Look at another year
1. Open [Holiday calendar](/hr/holidays).
2. Use the arrows beside the year to step back or forward, or **This year** to come back.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to the holiday calendar and the asset operations feature. Adding,
  editing and removing dates each need their own create, update and delete access.
- A time-off request that crosses a blackout is refused. A policy that charges every calendar day
  charges holidays too; only policies that skip weekends leave holidays out of the count.
- Removing a blackout lets workers request that date off again.
