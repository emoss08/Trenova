## What it's for
Review templates define what a performance review rates and how much each item counts toward
the overall score. HR administrators set them up; managers pick a template when they start a
review from a worker's **Reviews** tab. A template can repeat on a schedule, so closing a review
schedules the next one.

## Tasks

### Create a review template
Keywords: new review template, set up performance review
1. Open [Review templates](/hr/review-templates).
2. Select **New review template**.
3. Under **General**, fill in **Code**, **Name** and **Status**, and optionally **Repeat every**
   (in months), **Default template** and a **Description**.
4. Under **Rating items**, select **Add item** for each thing to score and fill in its **Key**,
   **Label**, **Weight** and optional **Hint**.
5. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** from the save button's menu.

### Edit a review template
1. Open [Review templates](/hr/review-templates).
2. Select the template's row, change the fields or rating items, and select **Save** or
   **Save & close**.

### Deactivate or restore templates
Keywords: archive review template, retire, reactivate
1. Open [Review templates](/hr/review-templates).
2. Tick the templates, then choose **Deactivate** or **Restore** in the bar at the bottom of the
   table. You can also right-click a single template.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to performance review templates and the asset operations feature.
  **Deactivate** and **Restore** only appear for people allowed to archive and restore templates.
- Rating items are copied onto every review when it starts. Changing a template only affects
  reviews started after you save; reviews in progress keep their items and weights.
- Only active templates with no open reviews can be deactivated.
- The overall score is the weighted average of the items, using each item's **Weight**. Leave
  **Repeat every** empty for one-off reviews.
