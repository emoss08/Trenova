## What it's for
Training courses is the catalog of courses workers can be put through. Each course records how
it is delivered and what passes, whether it is required and for which driver types, how long a
worker has to finish it, and how long a completion stays valid before a renewal is assigned.
Safety and HR administrators maintain the catalog; courses are assigned and completed on each
worker's **Training** tab.

## Tasks

### Add a training course
Keywords: new course, create training, add required course
1. Open [Training courses](/hr/training-courses).
2. Select **New training course**.
3. Under **General**, fill in **Code**, **Name**, **Category** and **Status**, and optionally a
   **Description**.
4. Under **Delivery**, choose the **Delivery** method and set **Duration**, **Link**,
   **Passing score** and **Driver acknowledges** as needed.
5. Under **Requirement & validity**, turn on **Required** if every matching worker must take it,
   pick the **Required for driver types**, and set **Due after**, **Valid for** and
   **Renewal alert window**.
6. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** from the save button's menu.

### Edit a training course
1. Open [Training courses](/hr/training-courses).
2. Select the course's row, change the fields, and select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Deactivate or restore courses
Keywords: retire course, archive training, reactivate
1. Open [Training courses](/hr/training-courses).
2. Tick the courses, then choose **Deactivate** or **Restore** in the bar at the bottom of the
   table. You can also right-click a single course.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to training courses and the asset operations feature.
  **Deactivate** and **Restore** only appear for people allowed to archive and restore courses.
- Required courses are assigned automatically on hire, rehire and driver-type change. An overdue,
  failed or lapsed required course leaves the worker unqualified.
- Leave every **Required for driver types** box unchecked to require the course for every worker.
  A **Due after** of 0 means no due date; leave **Valid for** empty for one-time courses.
- Only active courses with no open assignments can be deactivated.
