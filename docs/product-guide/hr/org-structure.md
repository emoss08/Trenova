## What it's for
Org structure shows the shape of the organization: the job positions the roster is counted by,
how they report to each other, how many people hold each, and who is approving in whose place.
Figures at the top give **People**, **Positions**, **Terminals** and **Cover in force**. The
**Org chart** lists positions as a tree with each one's headcount; the side panels break the
roster down **By terminal** and **By department** and list **Titles nobody holds yet**.

**Approval cover** lists approval delegations you have handed out or are covering for. HR and
managers use the page to set up positions, place people in them, and arrange cover while they
are away.

## Tasks

### Add a position
Keywords: new job title, create position, add role to org chart
1. Open [Org structure](/hr/org-structure).
2. Select **Add a position**, or the add button on a position's row to add one reporting to it.
3. Fill in **Code**, **Title**, **Department** and **Status**, and pick who it **Reports to**.
4. Turn on **Driving position** or **Exempt from overtime** where they apply, and add a
   **Description** if useful.
5. Select **Add**.

### Edit or move a position
Keywords: change reporting line, rename title, archive position
1. Open [Org structure](/hr/org-structure).
2. Open the menu on the position's row and choose **Edit**, change the fields, and select
   **Save**. Set **Status** to **Archived** to retire it; this is refused while anybody still
   holds it.
3. To change its reporting line, choose a position under **Move under** in the same menu, or
   **Nothing (top level)**. You can also drag the position onto another one.

### Put people in a position
Keywords: assign job title, who holds this position, remove from position
1. Open [Org structure](/hr/org-structure).
2. Select the position's headcount, or choose **Who holds it** from its menu.
3. Find the worker (or, for office positions, the user) and select **Add**.
4. To take someone off the position, use the remove button beside their name.

### Hand your approvals to a colleague
Keywords: delegate approvals, out of office cover, approval cover, vacation cover
1. Open [Org structure](/hr/org-structure) and find **Approval cover**.
2. Select **Arrange cover**.
3. Pick **Who is covering** and **What they can approve**, set **From** and **Until**, and give
   the reason in **Why**.
4. Select **Arrange**. To end it early, select **Call back** beside the cover.

## Notes
- The page appears only for organizations that run their own assets (asset operations) and needs
  read access to job positions. Adding positions needs create access; editing, moving and changing
  who holds a position need update access. **Approval cover** needs read access to approval
  delegations, and arranging cover needs create access.
- A delegation that is called back stays on the list so approvals made under it can still be
  explained.
