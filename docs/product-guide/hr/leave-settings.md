## What it's for
Leave settings is where HR sets how family and medical leave (FMLA) is measured for the whole
organization: which twelve-month period entitlements are counted against, how many weeks of leave
an eligible employee gets, how long a week is in hours, and what an employee needs to qualify.
The leave cases themselves are opened and tracked on each worker's **Leave** tab.

## Tasks

### Choose how the leave year is measured
Keywords: FMLA year, rolling year, calendar year, twelve-month period
1. Open [Leave settings](/hr/leave-settings).
2. Under **The twelve-month period**, pick **How the year is measured**: calendar year, twelve
   months from the hire anniversary, twelve months forward from first use, or rolling twelve
   months looking back.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Set the leave entitlement
Keywords: weeks of FMLA, military caregiver leave, hours per week
1. Open [Leave settings](/hr/leave-settings).
2. Under **What the entitlement is worth**, set **Weeks of leave**, **Military caregiver weeks**
   and **Hours in a workweek**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Set eligibility and certification rules
Keywords: FMLA eligibility, 1250 hours, 12 months service, medical certification deadline
1. Open [Leave settings](/hr/leave-settings).
2. Under **Eligibility and certification**, set **Months of service**,
   **Hours worked in the prior year** and **Days to return a certification**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to worker leave and the asset operations feature.
- The measurement method must be applied to every employee alike. Changing it is a change of
  policy: employees are entitled to sixty days' notice, and until then whichever method gives the
  greater benefit applies.
- Months of service is checked against the hire date. Hours worked cannot be counted
  automatically, so they are recorded by hand on each leave case.
- The statute's twelve weeks is a floor; you can set a more generous figure.
