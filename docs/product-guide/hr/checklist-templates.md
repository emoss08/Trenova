## What it's for
Checklist templates define the onboarding, offboarding and custom checklists run for workers.
Each template says which employment event starts it (when hired, rehired or terminated, or
started by hand), and lists the items to get done, with the team that owns each one and how
many days after the start it is due. HR administrators maintain the templates; the checklists
themselves are worked on each worker's **Checklist** tab.

## Tasks

### Create a checklist template
Keywords: new onboarding checklist, offboarding steps
1. Open [Checklist templates](/hr/checklist-templates).
2. Select **New checklist template**.
3. Under **General**, fill in **Code**, **Name**, **Kind**, **Starts** and **Status**, and
   optionally a **Description**.
4. Turn on **Default for this trigger** if this checklist should start automatically when the
   matching employment event is recorded.
5. Under **Items**, select **Add item** for each step and fill in its **Label**, **Kind**,
   **Owner**, **Due** (days after start) and **Required**. A document or credential item also
   asks for the **Document type** or **Credential type** that completes it.
6. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** from the save button's menu.

### Edit a checklist template
1. Open [Checklist templates](/hr/checklist-templates).
2. Select the template's row.
3. Change the fields or items and select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Deactivate or restore templates
Keywords: archive checklist, retire template, reactivate
1. Open [Checklist templates](/hr/checklist-templates).
2. Tick the templates, then choose **Deactivate** or **Restore** in the bar at the bottom of
   the table. You can also right-click a single template.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to checklist templates and the asset operations feature. Creating
  and editing need create and update access; **Deactivate** and **Restore** only appear for people
  allowed to archive and restore templates.
- Items are copied when a checklist starts, so editing a template does not change checklists
  already running.
- A template needs at least one item. A template started by hand cannot be the default; it is
  started from the worker's **Checklist** tab.
- Document and credential items complete themselves once the matching document or valid
  credential is on file.
