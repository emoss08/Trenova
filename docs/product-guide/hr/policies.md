## What it's for
Policies holds the handbooks and policies drivers and employees must read, and usually sign, from
Dash, the driver app. Each policy card shows its title, version, who it applies to (everyone,
employees or owner-operators), whether it needs a signature or is read only, and whether it is
retired. Figures at the top count policies **In force**, those that **Need a signature**, and
those **Retired**.

HR and safety managers publish policies here and check who still has to sign. A signature is tied
to the version it was given for, so publishing a new version asks everyone it applies to to read
and sign again.

## Tasks

### Publish a policy
Keywords: new policy, add handbook, upload handbook
1. Open [Policies](/hr/policies).
2. Select **Publish a policy**.
3. Fill in **Code**, **Title** and an optional **One-line summary**.
4. Under **The text**, choose **Write it here** and type the **Policy text**, or
   **Attach a document** and drop in a PDF or image.
5. Under **Who and when**, set the **Version**, **Applies to**, **Effective from** and **Status**,
   and turn on **Needs a signature** if people must sign it rather than just read it.
6. Select **Publish**. Everyone it applies to is asked to read (and sign) it from Dash.

### See who has signed a policy
Keywords: policy compliance, outstanding signatures, who has not signed
1. Open [Policies](/hr/policies).
2. Select **Who has signed** on the policy card.
3. Switch between **Outstanding**, **Signed** and **Everyone**, or find a person by name.

### Revise a policy
Keywords: update handbook, new version, correct policy text
1. Open [Policies](/hr/policies).
2. Hover over the policy card and select **Edit**.
3. To fix wording without asking for new signatures, change the text and keep the same
   **Version**. To make everyone sign again, give it a new **Version**.
4. Select **Save**.

### Retire a policy
Keywords: remove policy, withdraw handbook
1. Open [Policies](/hr/policies), hover over the card and select **Edit**.
2. Set **Status** to **Retired** and select **Save**.
3. Use **Everything** at the top of the list to see retired policies alongside those **In force**.

## Notes
- The page appears only for organizations that run their own assets (asset operations) and needs
  read access to worker policies. Publishing needs create access; editing needs update access.
- Retired policies are kept so older signatures still point at the policy they were given for.
