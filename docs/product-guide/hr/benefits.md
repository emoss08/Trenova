## What it's for
Benefits lists the plans the company offers for each plan year, grouped by type (medical, dental,
vision, life insurance, disability, retirement and other), and who is on each one. Each plan shows
how many people it covers and how many declined, and what the **Employee** and **Employer** pay per
settlement period. Figures at the top give **People covered**, **Plans on offer**,
**Employer puts in** and **Off settlements** (the employees' share, taken from their settlements).
The side panels list cover **Starting soon**, **Ending soon** and **Recently declined**.

HR and benefits administrators use it to set up and reprice plans. An employee's contribution is
taken through an ordinary settlement deduction.

## Tasks

### Add a benefit plan
Keywords: new plan, set up medical plan, create benefit
1. Open [Benefits](/hr/benefits).
2. Select **Add a plan**.
3. Fill in **Code**, **Name**, **Type**, **Plan year** and **Pay code** (the pay code the
   contribution is recorded under on settlements), and set **Status**.
4. Enter the **Employee cost per period** and **Employer cost per period**, and optionally the
   **Carrier**, **Policy number**, **Waiting period (days)** and **Description**.
5. Select **Add**.

### Change or archive a plan
Keywords: edit plan, reprice plan, retire plan
1. Open [Benefits](/hr/benefits) and pick the plan year if there is more than one.
2. Select the pencil button on the plan.
3. Change the fields, or set **Status** to **Archived** to stop offering it, then select **Save**.

### See who is on a plan
Keywords: plan roster, enrolled employees, who has coverage
1. Open [Benefits](/hr/benefits).
2. Select **Who is on it** on the plan.
3. Search the list by name or terminal.

### Enroll a worker or record that they declined
Keywords: enroll employee, add to plan, waive coverage, end coverage
1. Open [Workers](/hr/workers), select the worker and go to the **Pay** tab.
2. Under **Benefits**, select **Enroll or decline**.
3. Pick the **Plan**, the **Coverage** tier and **Effective from** date. Turn on
   **They declined the cover** to record a waiver instead, with the reason in **Why**.
4. Select **Enroll** (or **Record** for a waiver).
5. To stop someone's cover later, select **End cover** beside the plan on the same tab.

## Notes
- The page appears only for organizations that run their own assets (asset operations) and needs
  read access to benefit plans. Adding a plan needs create access, editing needs update access, and
  enrolling workers needs permission to assign benefit plans.
- Each plan year is priced separately, so repricing next year's plan never changes what someone
  was charged this year. To offer a plan in a new year, add a plan for that year.
- Ending a worker's cover stops their contribution; the record of the cover stays.
