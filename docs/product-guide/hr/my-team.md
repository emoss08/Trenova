## What it's for
My team is a manager's view of everyone they answer for: their own direct reports, the people at
the terminals they run, and anyone whose approvals have been handed to them. It shows how many are
in good standing (compliant, trained and rated Good or better), who needs attention, average
tenure, upcoming work anniversaries, a count by terminal, and any approval cover you currently
hold.

## Tasks

### See who needs attention
Keywords: overdue credentials, at-risk drivers, team problems
1. Open [My team](/hr/my-team).
2. Check **Needs your attention** for the people with problems, or select **Needs attention**
   above the **Roster** to show only them.
3. Select a person to open their worker record.

### Find someone on your team
Keywords: search my team, look up a report
1. Open [My team](/hr/my-team).
2. Type a name, title or terminal in the search box above the **Roster**.
3. Use **Everyone**, **Direct**, **Terminal** or **Covering** to narrow the list by how the
   person reaches you, and **Clear filters** to start again.
4. Turn on **Include people who have left** to see former team members too.

### Check whose approvals you are covering
Keywords: delegation, approval cover, acting manager
1. Open [My team](/hr/my-team).
2. Look at **Approval cover** for the managers whose approvals are in your hands and until when.
3. Select **Manage** to go to [Org structure](/hr/org-structure) and change delegations.

## Notes
- The page needs read access to workers and the asset operations feature. **Approval cover** only
  shows for people who can read approval delegations.
- A delegation never widens what you could approve on your own.
- If nobody reports to you, the page says **Nobody reports to you yet**.
