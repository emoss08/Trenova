## What it's for
Carrier settlement history is the read-only record of every carrier payable statement across all pay periods. Accounts payable and accounting staff use it to look up a past carrier settlement and see its lines, remittance details, rate confirmations, invoice matches and history.

The table shows **Status**, **Settlement #**, **Carrier**, **Period end**, **Pay date**, **Loads**, **Gross cost**, **Adjustments** and **Net payable**. Settlements are created and processed in the workspace, not here.

## Tasks

### Look up a carrier settlement
Keywords: find carrier statement, search carrier settlements
1. Open [Settlement history](/carrier-settlements/settlements).
2. Use the search box, or **Filter** and **Sort**, to narrow the list by carrier, status, period or pay date.
3. Select a row to open the settlement. It shows the **Gross cost**, **Adjustments**, **Loads** and **Net payable**, every line, the **Remittance** details, **Rate confirmations**, **Invoice matches** and the **History** of each status change.

### Work on a settlement that is still open
Keywords: process carrier settlement, pay carrier
1. Open [Settlement history](/carrier-settlements/settlements) and select the settlement.
2. Select **Open in workspace** to process, adjust or pay it in the [Workspace](/carrier-settlements/workspace). Paid and voided settlements are final and have no workspace link.

## Notes
Needs read access to carrier settlements, and the page is only available when the organization has the Brokerage capability.

Nothing can be submitted, approved, posted or paid from this page; those actions are in the workspace.
