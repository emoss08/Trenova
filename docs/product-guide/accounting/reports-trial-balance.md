## What it's for
The trial balance lists every general ledger account that had activity in a fiscal period, with
its **Account code**, **Account name**, **Category**, **Debit**, **Credit** and **Net change**, and
a **Totals** row at the bottom. Accountants use it to check that the books balance before closing a
period and to spot unusual account movements.

## Tasks

### Run the trial balance for a period
Keywords: view TB, period balances, month end check
1. Open [Trial balance](/accounting/reports/trial-balance).
2. Pick a **Fiscal year**, then a **Fiscal period**.
3. Read the account rows and compare the **Debit** and **Credit** totals in the **Totals** row.

## Notes
The report needs read access to accounting reports. If nothing was posted in the chosen period,
the page shows **Nothing posted**.
