## What it's for
AR aging shows how much each customer owes, split by how late it is: **Current**, 1–30, 31–60,
61–90 and 90+ days, with a **Total open** column and a totals row. Figures above the table give the
**Total open**, **Current** and **Overdue** amounts, **Current DSO** and **CEI**, with a
**Distribution** of the balance by age. Collections staff and controllers use it to see who owes
what and how old it is, and to export the aging for month-end.

## Tasks

### Review aging for all customers or one customer
Keywords: who owes us, aged balance, past due customers
1. Open [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging).
2. To narrow the list, pick a **Customer**. Leave it empty for all customers.
3. Select a column heading to sort by it.

### Run aging as of an earlier date
Keywords: historical aging, month end aging, aging as of date
1. Open [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging).
2. Set **As of date**. Leave it empty to age as of today.

### Export the aging to a spreadsheet
Keywords: download aging, CSV, Excel
1. Open [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging).
2. Set **Customer** and **As of date** if needed.
3. Select **Export CSV**. The file holds the rows currently shown.

### Drill into a customer's ledger
Keywords: customer detail, customer history
1. Open [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging).
2. Select the customer's name in the **Customer** column. Their
   [Customer ledger](/accounting/ar/customer-ledger) opens.

## Notes
Opening the page needs read access to accounts receivable.
