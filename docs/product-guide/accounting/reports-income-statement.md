## What it's for
The income statement shows revenue, cost of revenue and operating expenses for a fiscal period,
with **Gross profit** and **Net Income** totals. Controllers and owners use it to see whether the
business made money in a period.

## Tasks

### View the income statement for a period
Keywords: P&L, profit and loss, monthly results
1. Open [Income Statement](/accounting/reports/income-statement).
2. Pick a **Fiscal year**, then a **Fiscal period**.
3. Read the revenue, cost of revenue and operating expense sections, then **Gross profit** and
   **Net Income**.

## Notes
The report needs read access to accounting reports.
