## What it's for
The accounting dashboard is the landing page of Accounting management. It opens with the day's
brief, which leads with what the figures below it do not show: a customer who missed a promise to
pay, invoices about to pass 90 days past due, past-due customers nobody has contacted in two weeks,
promises and invoices coming due in the next few days, how much of the past-due balance one
customer holds, small short-paid balances that could be written off, invoices still sitting in
draft, and a book where no cash has been recorded at all. Under it, the next steps
open the page where each gets done. Below them are the figures at a
glance: **AR outstanding**, **Days sales outstanding**, **Collection effectiveness**,
**Unapplied cash** and the **Overdue** share, followed by the **Days sales outstanding** trend, a
**Cash-flow forecast**, an **Aging** breakdown, **Collections performance**, the
**Collections worklist** and the **Top overdue customers**, and the **Cash insights** found in the
book.

Controllers, AR clerks and collections staff use it to decide where to spend the day: which
customers to chase, how much cash is waiting to be applied, and how collections are trending.
Until the first invoice posts from billing, the page shows **Nothing on the books yet** instead of
the figures.

## Tasks

### Read the day's brief
Keywords: accounting brief, receivables summary, morning brief, what needs attention today, next steps, missed promise to pay, about to pass 90 days
1. Open [Accounting](/accounting). The brief is at the top of the page.
2. Select an underlined phrase to open what it names: a missed promise opens that customer in
   [Collections](/accounting/ar/collections), invoices about to pass 90 days open
   [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging), customers due a call open the due list in Collections, invoices
   falling due open [Open items](/accounting/ar/open-items), and a customer that holds much of the
   past-due balance opens in the [Customer ledger](/accounting/ar/customer-ledger).
3. Use the next steps under the brief to work through the day, most pressing first: call about a
   missed promise, call about invoices before they pass 90 days, contact customers not reached in
   14 days, post invoices still in draft, check that payments are being recorded, work the
   follow-ups that are due, confirm promises coming due, send reminders or statements for invoices
   falling due, clear small short-pay balances and apply unapplied cash.
4. The line under the brief says when it was written, and is marked with the assist mark when an
   AI provider worded it from the computed figures. Without a provider the brief reads plainer and
   says the same thing.

### Check how receivables are doing
Keywords: DSO, days sales outstanding, AR balance, CEI, collection effectiveness, overdue percent
1. Open [Accounting](/accounting).
2. Read the figures across the top: **AR outstanding**, **Days sales outstanding**,
   **Collection effectiveness**, **Unapplied cash** and **Overdue**.
3. Select **AR outstanding** to go to [AR aging](/accounting/ar/aging), **Unapplied cash** to go to
   [Customer payments](/accounting/ar/payments), or **Overdue** to go to
   [Open items](/accounting/ar/open-items).

### See which customers to chase
Keywords: collections, past due customers, worklist, who owes us
1. Open [Accounting](/accounting).
2. Look at the **Collections worklist** and **Top overdue customers**. Worklist rows show how far
   past due an invoice is and flag **Disputed** and **Short-paid** invoices.
3. Select a worklist row to open that customer in [Collections](/accounting/ar/collections), or a
   top overdue customer to open it in the [Customer ledger](/accounting/ar/customer-ledger).
4. Select **Open collections** to work the whole queue, or **View aging** to see every customer's
   balance by age.

### Switch the aging view
Keywords: aging chart, aging trend, aging buckets
1. Open [Accounting](/accounting).
2. In the **Aging** panel, switch between **Snapshot** and **Trend** in the panel header. The trend
   view adds a range of 13, 26 or 52 weeks beside it.

### Record a customer payment from the dashboard
Keywords: receive payment, cash receipt, apply cash
1. Open [Accounting](/accounting).
2. Select **Record payment** in the page header. This opens the record payment panel on
   [Customer payments](/accounting/ar/payments).

## Notes
Opening the dashboard needs read access to accounts receivable. **Record payment** only appears for
people who can create customer payments.

The brief is written for the organization before the workday and again when the figures it cites
change, checked at most every half hour and a few times a day at most, so two people reading it
see the same words.

The other accounting pages, such as [Manual journals](/accounting/manual-journals),
[Journal reversals](/accounting/journal-reversals),
[Bank receipts](/accounting/reconciliation/bank-receipts),
[Trial balance](/accounting/reports/trial-balance) and
[Balance sheet](/accounting/reports/balance-sheet), are in the Accounting panel beside the page.
