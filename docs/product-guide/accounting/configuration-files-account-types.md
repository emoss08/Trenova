## What it's for
Account types group your general ledger accounts under an accounting category. Each account type
has a **Code**, a **Name**, a **Status**, a **Category** (**Asset**, **Liability**, **Equity**,
**Revenue**, **Cost of Revenue** or **Expense**), and an optional **Description** and **Color**.
The table shows **Status**, **Code**, **Name**, **Category**, **Description** and **Created At**.

Accounting administrators maintain the list when setting up or extending the chart of accounts.

## Tasks

### Add an account type
Keywords: new account type, create account category
1. Open [Account types](/accounting/configuration-files/account-types).
2. Select **New account type**.
3. Fill in **Status**, **Code** (up to 10 characters), **Name** and **Category**. These are
   required.
4. Optionally add a **Description** and a **Color**.
5. Select **Save**, or choose **Save & close** or **Save & add another** from the save button's
   menu.

### Edit an account type
Keywords: rename account type, change category
1. Open [Account types](/accounting/configuration-files/account-types).
2. Find it with the search box or **Filter**, then select its row.
3. Change the fields and select **Save** or **Save & close**.

### Activate or deactivate account types
Keywords: retire account type, inactive
1. Open [Account types](/accounting/configuration-files/account-types).
2. To change one, select the status badge in its **Status** column and pick the new status.
3. To change several, tick the rows, choose **Update status** in the bar that appears and pick
   **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Notes
Opening the page needs read access to account types. Adding needs create access; editing and
changing status need update access.
