## What it's for
The balance sheet shows assets, liabilities and equity as they stood at the close of a fiscal
period, with **Total assets**, **Total liabilities** and **Total equity**, plus
**Current year earnings** when there are any. A check at the bottom compares assets with
liabilities plus equity and reads **Balance sheet is balanced** or **Balance sheet is NOT balanced**.

## Tasks

### View the balance sheet for a period
Keywords: assets, liabilities, equity, check books balance
1. Open [Balance sheet](/accounting/reports/balance-sheet).
2. Pick a **Fiscal year**, then a **Fiscal period**.
3. Read the asset, liability and equity sections and check the balance line at the bottom.

## Notes
The report needs read access to accounting reports. If the check reads
**Balance sheet is NOT balanced**, the [Trial balance](/accounting/reports/trial-balance) for the
same period is a good place to start looking.
