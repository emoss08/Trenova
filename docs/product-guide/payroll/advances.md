## What it's for
Pay advances records cash and money-code advances given to drivers on the road, such as an EFS or Comdata money code, cash or a fuel card advance. The outstanding balance of each advance is recovered automatically from the driver's next settlements, and this page tracks how much has been recovered and how much is still owed.

The table shows each advance's **Status** (outstanding, partially recovered, recovered or written off), **Driver**, **Source**, **Reference**, **Issued** date, **Amount**, **Recovered** and **Outstanding**.

## Tasks

### Record an advance
Keywords: issue advance, new money code, give driver cash
1. Open [Pay advances](/payroll/advances).
2. Select **New pay advance**.
3. Choose the **Driver** and the **Source**, and enter the **Reference** (the money code or check number).
4. Enter the **Issued date** (the day the driver received the funds) and the **Amount**, and add **Notes** on what it covered.
5. Select **Save**.

### Check what a driver still owes
Keywords: advance balance, recovered amount
1. Open [Pay advances](/payroll/advances).
2. Search or use **Filter** to find the driver's advances.
3. Select an advance to see its **Amount**, **Recovered** and **Outstanding** balance.

### Write off an advance
Keywords: forgive advance, uncollectible advance, terminated driver
1. Open [Pay advances](/payroll/advances) and select the advance.
2. Select **Write off remaining balance**, enter the reason, and confirm with **Write off**.
3. To write off several at once, tick them and select **Write off** in the bar that appears, then enter one reason and confirm.

## Notes
Needs read access to pay advances to open the page; recording and writing off need the matching permissions. The page is only available when the organization has the Asset operations capability.

Only outstanding or partially recovered advances can be written off. A written-off balance is no longer recovered from future settlements, and the reason is shown on the advance.

A driver's outstanding advances also appear in the right rail of the [Workspace](/payroll/workspace).
