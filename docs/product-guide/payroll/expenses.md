## What it's for
Driver expenses lists the out-of-pocket expenses drivers submit from the driver portal (Dash). Payroll staff check each receipt, then approve the expense to reimburse it on the driver's open settlement, or reject it with an explanation.

The table shows **Status** (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Reimbursed or Cancelled), **Driver**, **Amount**, **Description**, **Incurred** date, whether a **Receipt** is attached, and the **Submitted** and **Reviewed** dates. Expenses are submitted by drivers, so there is no create button on this page.

## Tasks

### Review an expense and its receipt
Keywords: check receipt, open expense
1. Open [Driver expenses](/payroll/expenses).
2. Use the search box, or **Filter** on **Status**, to find pending expenses.
3. Select the expense to see the amount, what the driver wrote, the pay code and the receipt. Select **View receipt** to open it.

### Approve and reimburse an expense
Keywords: approve expense, reimburse driver, pay back driver
1. Open [Driver expenses](/payroll/expenses) and select a pending expense.
2. Under **Review this expense**, keep **Approve & reimburse** selected.
3. Optionally add a **Note (optional)**, then select **Approve expense**.

### Reject an expense
Keywords: deny expense, decline reimbursement
1. Open [Driver expenses](/payroll/expenses) and select a pending expense.
2. Select **Reject**, enter the **Rejection reason**, and select **Reject expense**.

## Notes
Needs read access to driver expenses to open the page, and the page is only available when the organization has the Asset operations capability.

Approving immediately adds a reimbursement line to the driver's open settlement; if the driver has none, an off-cycle draft settlement is created. The rejection reason is shown to the driver word for word.

Only pending expenses can be reviewed. Once reviewed, the panel shows the note, who reviewed it and when, and whether the reimbursement was applied to a settlement.
