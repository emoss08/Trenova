## What it's for
Recurring earnings are standing amounts added to a driver's settlements, such as per diem, safety and performance bonuses, stipends and equipment rental payments. Each one belongs to one driver, uses an earning pay code, and is added automatically every settlement or once a month until it is paused, reaches its end date, or hits its total cap.

The table shows each earning's **Status**, **Driver**, **Code**, **Description**, **Frequency**, **Amount** and **Paid / cap**.

## Tasks

### Add a recurring earning
Keywords: new earning, set up per diem, add bonus, add stipend
1. Open [Recurring earnings](/payroll/earnings).
2. Select **New recurring earning**.
3. Choose the **Driver**, the **Pay code** and the **Frequency** (every settlement, or monthly on the first settlement of each month).
4. Enter a **Description** and the **Amount per application**. Optionally set a **Total cap**, after which the earning stops on its own.
5. Set the **Start date** and, if it should end, an **End date**.
6. Select **Save**.

### Edit an earning
Keywords: change earning amount
1. Open [Recurring earnings](/payroll/earnings) and select the earning.
2. Change the fields, including **Status**, then select **Save**.

### Pause or resume earnings
Keywords: skip earning, stop bonus temporarily
1. Open [Recurring earnings](/payroll/earnings) and tick the earnings.
2. Select **Update status**, then **Pause** or **Resume**.

## Notes
Needs read access to recurring earnings to open the page; creating and editing need the matching permissions. The page is only available when the organization has the Asset operations capability.

Paused earnings are skipped on future settlements and keep their history. A completed earning has reached its cap and has stopped permanently.

Earnings can also be paused or resumed from the right rail of the [Workspace](/payroll/workspace).
