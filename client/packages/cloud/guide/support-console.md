## What it's for
The support console is where Trenova platform staff open a support session in an organization that
allowed Trenova support access. It lists **Organizations allowing support access** with the
**Access** each one allowed and when it ends. Anyone who is not Trenova platform staff sees
**Trenova staff only**.

## Tasks

### Open a support session
Keywords: help a customer, look at a customer's data, start support session
1. Sign in with your authenticator code. Without a two-factor sign-in the console explains what to do
   and sessions cannot be opened.
2. Open the [Support console](/support-console) and select **Open support session** beside the
   organization.
3. Give the **Ticket** and the **Reason**, then select **Open support session**. The app reloads inside
   the organization with the support session banner across the top.

### Make a change for the customer
Keywords: elevate, write access, fix data for a customer
1. In the banner select **Elevate to write**. The organization must have allowed read-write access.
2. Enter your **Password**, your **Authentication code**, the **Ticket** and what you will change,
   then select **Elevate to write**. Write access lasts thirty minutes; select **Return to read-only**
   to end it sooner.

### Leave a support session
Keywords: exit support session, stop helping, return to staff organization
1. Select **Exit** in the banner. You return to the [Support console](/support-console).

## Notes
A support session ends on its own when its time runs out, when the organization revokes access, when
you sign out, or when you are removed from platform staff, and the console says why. Opening another
session ends the one you had open.
