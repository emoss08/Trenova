## 0. Context & Decisions (do not lose this)

### Thesis
Every other TMS treats detention as a **billing output** computed at invoice time.
Trenova treats it as a **receivable that forms in real time and must be defended**.
Three consequences: (1) live not retrospective, (2) every charge carries its own
proof, (3) the policy is a model you can backtest before betting money on it.

### Locked product decisions
| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| A | Replace vs layer | **Replace.** New `DetentionPolicy` is the single source of truth. `ShipmentControl` detention fields demote to org-default fallback. Dead `CustomerBillingProfile` detention fields are dropped. |
| B | Occurrence snapshot | **Snapshot.** Each occurrence freezes the policy terms in force, so disputes replay the real contract. |
| C | Notification | **Send AND gate.** Trenova generates + sends the notice, tracks delivery, and can suppress billing when the required notice was missed. |
| D | Scope dimensions | Customer, **Location (facility)**, ShipmentType, ServiceType, Commodity, StopType. Explicit precedence + "why this policy won" explainer. |
| E | Driver pay free time | **Co-located** on the policy; pay profile inherits when policy value is null. |
| F | Client surfaces | Detention Desk (live board), Policy builder w/ live preview, Shipment detention tab w/ calculation receipt. |

   LIVE: fires `detention_candidate` notification
   (`internal/core/services/shipmentmoveservice/stopactuals.go:122`).
3. `driverpay.PayProfileComponent` — `ComponentKindDetention`, `PerHour` only, own free time.
   LIVE: `detentionHours()` in `internal/core/services/driversettlementservice/calculator.go:273`.
4. `customer.CustomerBillingProfile` — `DetentionBillingEnabled`, `DetentionFreeMinutes`,
   `DetentionRatePerHour`, `CountDetentionOnlyOnAppointmentStops`.
   **DEAD**: reachable only via GraphQL resolvers + Zod schemas. Zero business logic reads them.

### Confirmed defects to fix
- [x] **D1** `CustomerBillingProfile.DetentionFreeMinutes` is `int8` (max 127). A 3-hour
      (180 min) free window cannot round-trip. DB column is `SMALLINT` so storage is fine;
      the Go type is the bug. No clamping or validation anywhere.
- [x] **D2** `syncDetentionCharge` recomputes from `c.now()` on every pass, so an in-progress
      stop's charge silently drifts and nothing records why a number was produced.
- [x] **D3** `CountDetentionOnlyOnAppointmentStops` never read despite `Stop.ScheduleType`
      (`Open`/`Appointment`) existing and making it trivially enforceable.
- [x] **D4** Three unsynchronized thresholds (bill 30 / alert 120 / pay N).

### Market research findings that drive the model
- Free time: **2 hours** standard per stop; often different for load vs unload.
- Billing rate $50–85/hr to customer; driver detention pay $20–50/hr. Spread = margin.
- **Tiered escalation** is real and growing: $75/hr first 2 hrs, then $100/hr.
- **Daily caps** (~$500/day) — TRAP: a stay spanning midnight can generate two daily
  charges. Cap scope must be explicit (per stop / per calendar day / per shipment).
- **Clock start**: actual arrival | appointment regardless of arrival | later-of.
- **Late arrival can VOID detention entirely** — common shipper-favorable clause.
  Requires 4-way enum, not a boolean.
- **Clock stop**: unload complete per POD — NOT gate-out, NOT driver's check-in call.
- Rounding: 15/30/60-min increments, up/down/nearest. "2h10m billed as 3h" is a
  named dispute cause.
- **~1 in 5 detention invoices contains a billing error.**
- Dispute evidence set: timestamped arrival, timestamped departure, **written notice at
  or before free-time expiry**, signed BOL, rate confirmation, invoice detail.
- Detention → layover boundary at ~24h (different charge type).
- McLeod: separates customer billing from driver pay; has "warning time" inside free time.
- Trimble TMW: accessorial rate tables with "restrictions" matched against the trip.

### Precedence / specificity scoring (deterministic)
Resolution order: `Priority DESC, SpecificityScore DESC, CreatedAt ASC`.
SpecificityScore computed in `BeforeAppendModel`:

| Scope field set | Weight |
|---|---|
| LocationID | 32 |
| CustomerID | 16 |
| CommodityIDs non-empty | 8 |
| ServiceTypeIDs non-empty | 4 |
| ShipmentTypeIDs non-empty | 2 |
| StopTypes non-empty | 1 |

Max 63. Surfaced in the UI as the "why this policy won" explainer.
